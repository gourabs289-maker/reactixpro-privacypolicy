Privacy Policy for Reactix Pro

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in Reactix Pro ("the App"). The App is provided as a free service and is intended for use "as is". By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a fully functioning laboratory simulator, Reactix Pro requires specific device permissions. The App does not collect or store your personal identity data on our servers. The permissions requested include:

  • Camera Permission: Required to scan chemical diagrams and molecular structures using Optical Character Recognition (OCR). Images captured by the camera are processed to solve equations and are not stored permanently by us.
  • Read/Write External Storage: Required to access your device's gallery so you can upload images of chemical equations for analysis, and to save generated PDF reports (such as reaction results and stoichiometry data) directly to your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (AdMob)

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Specifically, we use Google AdMob to serve advertisements.

Google AdMob uses mobile advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your usage and interests. To learn more about how Google handles your data, please review their privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy

3. External APIs (Google Gemini)

Reactix Pro utilizes the Google Gemini API to process and solve chemical equations, provide astrochemistry simulations, and analyze images uploaded via the OCR scanner. Data sent to this API is strictly limited to the text queries or image data you input into the App for scientific processing.

4. Log Data and Analytics

In the case of an error or app crash, we may collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the App, the time and date of your use, and other statistics to help us fix the issue.
